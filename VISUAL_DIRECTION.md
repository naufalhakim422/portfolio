## 1. Core Creative Direction

**Philosophy: Creative Developer × Creative Technologist × Sky Blue Atmosphere**
The portfolio is designed around the concept of **"Quiet Confidence in Midnight Blue"** — transitioning away from harsh flat pitch-black into an atmospheric, deep celestial midnight slate canvas enriched with refined **Sky Blue (`#38bdf8`)** accents and soft luminous depth.

### Core Attributes
* **Atmospheric Midnight Canvas**: Deep celestial slate base with subtle radial sky-blue ambient lighting.
* **Refined Sky Blue Accents**: Strategic illumination using Sky 400 (`#38bdf8`) and Sky 300 (`#7dd3fc`) for key focal points, badges, magnetic triggers, and cursor states.
* **Minimalist & Architectural**: Generous whitespace, asymmetric 12-column layouts, and thin 1px structural hairline borders.
* **Purposeful Motion**: Smooth, cinematic easing (`power3.out`, `expo.out`) that serves comprehension and visual storytelling.
